Expected formats

Habit sheet: a header row containing DAY, DATE, then one column per habit with TRUE/FALSE values per day. Dates like 7/21 or 7/21/26.

Journal doc: one block per day, like:

07/24/26 | 8 | Cambridge

10:30 | Call with Lydia | Home | 8
3:00 | Softball w/ Cory | Field | 9

That's date | day score (0–10) | city, then one line per activity: time | title | location (optional) | rating (0–10). Bullets are fine too.

Adjust your Sheet/Doc (or the links above), then hit Re-check.
