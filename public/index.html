Suggested formats

Habit sheet: a header row containing DATE (and optionally DAY), then one column per habit with TRUE/FALSE per day.

Journal doc: a date line starting each day, then one line per activity:

07/24/26 | 8 | Cambridge
10:30 | Call with Lydia | Home | 8
3:00 | Softball w/ Cory | Field | 9

That's date | day score (0–10) | city, then time | title | location | rating. Parsing is deliberately loose — bullets are fine, the date can be 7/24, 2026-07-24 or July 24, 2026, times can be 9:30am or 21:00, ratings can be 8 or 8/10, and |, tabs or - all work as separators. Location and rating are optional.
